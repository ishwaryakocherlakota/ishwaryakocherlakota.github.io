    Portfolio Case Study

    SampleFlow: Pre-Analytical Lab Workbench

    Problem Statement: Over 60% of clinical laboratory errors occur in the pre-analytical phase—wrong anticoagulant tubes, hemolyzed samples, and missed STAT turnaround times (TAT) caused by cluttered legacy LIS interfaces.

    1. WCAG 1.4.1 Dual-Coding

    Every vacutainer cap pairs color with a distinct geometric symbol (● Lavender, ■ Blue, ▲ Gold, ◆ Green, ✚ Gray) so colorblind technicians never rely on hue alone.

    2. Gloved-Hand Ergonomics

    Toggling Gloved 56px Mode expands primary touch targets to ≥56px height to accommodate double-gloved bench operation on touch terminals.

    3. Automated Mismatch Interception

    Try clicking Scan Next Vial—the first simulated scan catches a Fluoride Gray tube scanned for STAT Electrolytes (which requires Heparin Green).

    4. 2-Tap QC & NABL Audit Trail

    Standardized rejection chips eliminate free-text bottlenecks and generate an instant timestamped CSV report for lab accreditation audits.
